Privacy Policy — Newood Bookkeeping Automation

Effective date: October 24, 2026

Newood Bookkeeping Automation (the "App") is a private, internal application developed and used solely by Newood Millwork Inc. ("Newood", "we", "us") to automate Newood's own bookkeeping in QuickBooks Online. The App is not offered, sold, or distributed to any other business or individual.

Data the App accesses

Through the QuickBooks Online API, the App accesses only Newood Millwork Inc.'s own QuickBooks Online company data, which may include: transactions (bills, expenses, invoices, payments), vendor and customer records contained in those transactions, attachments, and financial reports. The App accesses this data solely so Newood can enter, attach, reconcile, and report on its own books.

How the data is used

The data is used exclusively for Newood's internal accounting operations. It is never sold, rented, leased, or shared with third parties, and it is never used for advertising or marketing.

Data storage and security

Accounting data remains in QuickBooks Online. The App stores only the technical credentials (OAuth tokens) required to connect to QuickBooks Online; these are kept in secure credential storage and are accessible only to authorized Newood personnel and its authorized automation systems. All API communication takes place over encrypted (HTTPS/TLS) connections.

Data retention

Connection credentials are retained only while the App is in use by Newood and are revoked and deleted when the App is disconnected or decommissioned.

Your choices

Because the App is used only by Newood Millwork Inc. for its own data, there are no external end users. The connection between the App and QuickBooks Online can be disconnected at any time from within QuickBooks Online (Apps > My Apps) or the Intuit Developer portal.
