Newood Bookkeeping Automation

Newood Bookkeeping Automation is a private, internal application used solely by Newood Millwork Inc. to automate its own bookkeeping in QuickBooks Online. It is not offered to other businesses or individuals, and there is no public sign-up.

If you are an authorized Newood Millwork Inc. user and need to connect or reconnect the app, please contact the administrator at jasonsong@newoodmillwork.com. To disconnect the app, open QuickBooks Online and go to Apps > My Apps, or contact the administrator.
